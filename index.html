About Us

A Trusted Name Since 1999

Established in 1999, SURYA TRADERS has been a trusted name in the dry fruits industry, serving customers with premium-quality products for over 25 years. As a leading wholesale and retail supplier, we are committed to delivering freshness, purity, and exceptional value to households, retailers, businesses, and distributors.

Our Mission

To provide premium-quality dry fruits and nuts at competitive prices while maintaining the highest standards of freshness, hygiene, and customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted and preferred dry fruit wholesale and retail brands by consistently delivering superior quality products and exceptional customer service.

Our Promise

At SURYA TRADERS, quality is not just a promise—it's our tradition. Every product is carefully selected, quality-tested, and packed to preserve its natural taste, freshness, and nutritional value. We are dedicated to building long-lasting relationships with our customers through honesty, reliability, and excellence.
